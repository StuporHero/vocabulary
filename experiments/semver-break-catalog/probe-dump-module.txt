Legend:
  ok     = digest verdict agrees with empirical outcome.
  ok-err = library no longer compiles; either digest signals it.
  MISS   = false negative — digest matches when consumer breaks.
           Lets a real major change ship as minor. Dangerous.
  OVER   = false positive — digest differs when consumer doesn't
           break. For a publish-time tool this forces a major
           bump that wasn't needed, which trains authors to
           override or ignore the tool. Not 'just nuisance.'
